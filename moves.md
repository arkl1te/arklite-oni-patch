# Moves

Every hand-to-hand move of the characters in the F2 menu (Arklite's default roster), read from the game's own animation data (level0). Each character's moves come from its animation set and the sets it inherits (listed under its name); a move the character's own set has wins over an inherited one.

Names are the game's own only: Konoko's move list from her diary, the name a character shouts with a super move, and Tanker's move list from the Oni wiki. A move that shares an animation with a named one keeps that name, even on another character. Moves without a name have none in the game.

Damage is the move's base damage before multiplayer's ×1.25, summed over all its hits; throws do their damage through the thrown character's fall, so they show —. *Unblockable* hits can't be blocked.

Inputs: *forward/back/left/right* are the movement keys. "Crouch, let go of crouch, punch" means press crouch, release it and press punch right away. "Back, then forward + kick" means tap back, then press forward and kick together.

For everyone: no key blocks (stand still facing the attacker; crouch for low attacks), and crouch + any direction is an escape roll (the diary's Escape Moves). Up close against an armed opponent, the throws with forward + punch or forward + kick take the weapon (the diary's Disarm and Stepping Disarm).
